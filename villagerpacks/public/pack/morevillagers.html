About More Villagers

More Villagers is a villager pack for VillagerAPI on Minecraft 26.3 with Fabric or NeoForge. It adds 10 villager professions, 1 villager type and 264 custom villager trades, plus new village houses for Badlands, Desert, Plains, Savanna, Snowy and Taiga villages. Every profession has its own workstation, so unemployed villagers pick up the new jobs just like vanilla ones.

Villager professions

  • Enderologist works at the Purpur Altar and has 25 trades across 5 levels.
  • Engineer works at the Blueprint Table and has 24 trades across 5 levels.
  • Explorer works at the Decorated Pot and has 21 trades across 5 levels.
  • Florist works at the Gardening Table and has 34 trades across 5 levels.
  • Hunter works at the Hunting Post and has 24 trades across 5 levels.
  • Iceman works at the Chiller and has 22 trades across 5 levels.
  • Miner works at the Mining Bench and has 25 trades across 5 levels.
  • Netherologist works at the Decayed Workbench and has 26 trades across 5 levels.
  • Oceanographer works at the Oceanography Table and has 28 trades across 5 levels.
  • Forester works at the Forestry Bench and has 35 trades across 5 levels.

Villager types

  • Badlands villagers live in Badlands, Wooded Badlands and Eroded Badlands.

Make your own

Want different trades or another profession? Load this pack, or the mod jar it comes in, into the villager pack generator, change anything and download your own pack. No code needed.
